# Sabarmati STP budgets — notes

## What this file is

One budget row per STP (24 rows), in the Yamuna `stp_budgets.csv` format.

## The headline finding: 0 of 24 disclosed

**No sanctioned cost, funds released/utilised, contractor name, award date,
scheduled/actual completion, cost overrun or time overrun figure is published
for ANY of the 24 plants** in the sources used (Gujarat MPRs April 2020 and
April 2021, CPCB National Inventory March 2021, NMCG CMC minutes, World Bank
ESIA 2022). Every row has `budget_status: "undisclosed"` and the money
columns are blank. Missing disclosure is itself a finding — the Ganga
accountability deep-dive found the same pattern (funds released/utilised
disclosed for 0 of 145 plants).

## Project-level figures that exist but are NOT per-plant

These figures are real, but they belong to projects, not to individual plants
in this file, and they are **never summed** into a per-plant total:

- **New 375 MLD STP at Vasna, Ahmedabad: ₹840 crore** — tender (eProcure,
  September 2023). This is a NEW plant, not the existing Vasna 126 MLD plant.
  Noted on the Vasna 126 MLD row.
- **Old Pirana 200 MLD + 150 MLD tertiary treatment plant: ₹720 crore** —
  HAM concession with 15-year O&M; tender, bids closing 29 August 2026 (not
  yet awarded). Noted on the Pirana rows.
- **Ahmedabad deep-sea disposal pipeline (300 MLD): ₹1,478 crore** —
  Government Resolution 26/11/2019, Gujarat Water Infrastructure Ltd (GWIL)
  executing. Jetpur 80 MLD pipeline: ₹669 crore. These remove the industrial
  footprint from the Sabarmati permanently; they are not STP costs.
- **₹282.17 crore spent on Sabarmati cleaning in the past 3 years** — Rajya
  Sabha, July 2022. A state programme figure, not attributable to any plant.

## What would close this gap

- NMCG / Gujarat Urban Development Department project sanction orders for the
  Vasna and Pirana upgrades (these would give sanctioned cost, contractor
  and timelines for the new plants).
- AMC / AUDA annual budgets or audit reports for the existing 18 Ahmedabad
  plants.
- The Gujarat High Court PIL C/WPPIL/98/2021 record (2024) — the AMC
  affidavit and GPCB reports in that case may contain cost and compliance
  figures not yet extracted.
