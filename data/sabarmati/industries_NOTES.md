# Sabarmati industries — notes

## What this file is

One aggregate row: the **7 Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETPs) in
Ahmedabad**, total consented/design capacity **120.88 MLD** (Gujarat MPR,
April 2020). The row is plotted at the Ahmedabad town centroid.

## Why one aggregate row

The MPR names the CETP expansion projects (GECSL 16→35 MLD, NEPL 3→14 MLD,
OEPL spray dryer, NDES up to 0.45 MLD, NTIEM 100→130 MLD, new AHSPA CETP
30 MLD at Danilimda, due Dec 2021) but **does not publish the 7 CETPs'
individual capacities or coordinates**, so no per-CETP rows can be built.
There are 1,351 industries in the Danilimda–Behrampura belt (1,066 CETP
members, 285 with individual ETPs).

## The permanent solution

The deep-sea disposal pipeline (Ahmedabad 300 MLD, ₹1,478 crore, GR
26/11/2019, GWIL executing; Jetpur 80 MLD, ₹669 crore) is the planned
permanent solution to remove the industrial footprint from the Sabarmati.
The CMC minutes of January 2023 record that downstream of Vasna Barrage the
major issue is industrial, and that 5,538 industrial connections had been
removed.

## What the effluent figure is — and is not

120.88 MLD is a **consented/design capacity** figure, never a measured
discharge. It is not added to any drain's BOD load, and it must not be
treated as a pollution measurement. No per-industry effluent quality data is
published.

## Gaps

- No public geocoded list of grossly polluting industries (GPIs) on the
  Sabarmati exists (the same gap as on the Yamuna, where the file holds
  aggregates only).
- No individual CETP capacities, locations or outlet quality published.
- The Khari/Meshwo tributary industrial belt (eastern Ahmedabad) has no
  published drain or industry inventory.
