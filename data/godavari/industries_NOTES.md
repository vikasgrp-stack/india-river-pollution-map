# Godavari industries — notes

## What this file is

10 rows of grossly polluting industries / CETPs in the Godavari basin,
as aggregate rows only where a published source supports them. Effluent
figures are consented/design capacities, never measurements, and are
never added to any drain's load.

## Coverage

- **2 rows carry a figure (41.53 MLD total)**: Waluj CETP,
  Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, 10 MLD; Andhra Paper Ltd, Rajahmundry,
  31.53 MLD.
- **8 rows are aggregates/belts with no figure**: Nashik Ambad–Satpur
  MIDC belt; Nanded MIDC estate (Vasarni CETP); Ramagundam industrial
  belt (NTPC, RFCL, Singareni, cement); Enumamula tanneries CETP,
  Warangal (non-operational); ITC Paperboards, Bhadrachalam;
  Mancherial–Bellampalli coal belt (Singareni mines, STPP); Kothagudem
  Thermal Power Station, Paloncha; Chandrapur–Ballarpur industrial belt
  (Wardha).

## Sources

- Maharashtra MPR Jun 2020; Telangana MPRs Sep–Oct 2020; AP MPR Dec
  2022 (source PDFs + extracted text kept in work/industries/).
- CPCB CETP assessment (source PDF + text kept).

## Exclusions — verified, not assumed

- **Patancheru CETP (7.5 MLD) EXCLUDED** — since July 2009 its effluent
  goes to the Musi, not the Manjira/Godavari.
- **Akola / Washim / Amravati CETP EXCLUDED** — the Purna through Akola
  is a Tapi tributary, not the Godavari's Purna (MPCB Purna action plan
  2019).
- **No CETP at all in the Andhra Pradesh Godavari stretch.**
- **No Godavari equivalent of the Yamuna GPI inspection exists** —
  CPCB/NMCG's annual GPI drive covers Ganga + Yamuna only.

## Gaps

- No per-industry effluent measurements published anywhere in the basin;
  consented capacities exist for 2 rows only.
- No Chhattisgarh/Odisha industry data obtained.
