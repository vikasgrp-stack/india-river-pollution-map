# Godavari towns — notes

## What this file is

87 towns along the Godavari main stem and tributaries (Maharashtra 41,
Telangana 37, Andhra Pradesh 7, Puducherry 1, Chhattisgarh 1), with Census
2011 population and the current MLA (name + party + constituency +
election year) for every town. 34 rows from the main towns research
(Tier 1 main stem + Tier 2 tributary towns); 53 rows from the
supplementary research covering every other town that has an STP or drain
row. Featured towns (6): Nashik, Nanded, Nizamabad, Rajahmundry, Warangal,
Kakinada.

## Coverage

- **Towns with population: 87 of 87, summed 12,748,996** (all Census 2011
  city/municipal figures; no town left blank).
- **Towns with MLA: 87 of 87** — every MLA verified against MyNeta
  winners lists, ECI gazette notifications, or Wikipedia constituency
  articles citing ECI results. None guessed.
- **Literacy blank for 55 towns, sex ratio blank for 29, area blank for
  40** — only source-stated figures used; taluka/mandal areas never
  substituted. **Urban/rural split blank for all 87** (not compiled).
- **No religion shares, no 20-year ruling history, no livelihood splits**
  — not compiled for Godavari towns (same convention as Sabarmati/Yamuna);
  livelihoods are qualitative only.

## Corrections found during verification

- **Trimbakeshwar is NOT in Nashik West** — it falls in **Igatpuri
  (ST)**, MLA Hiraman Bhika Khoskar (NCP, 2024). Nashik West covers only
  Nashik city wards.
- **There is no Dharmabad assembly constituency** — Dharmabad town is in
  **Naigaon** AC (MLA Rajesh Sambhaji Pawar, BJP, 2024).
- **Yanam's 2021 frame is out of date** — Puducherry held a fresh election
  in **May 2026**: current MLA is **Malladi Krishna Rao (AINRC, 2026)**,
  not the 2021 winner Gollapalli Srinivas Ashok (IND, now INC, lost by
  4,568). Verified via the ECI notification (CEO Puducherry), MyNeta
  Puducherry 2026 winners, IndiaVotes and Wikipedia.
- **Akola and Washim dropped at assembly** — they sit on the Purna that
  is a Tapi tributary, not the Godavari's Purna (verified: Wikipedia
  disambiguation + MPCB Purna action plan 2019 + NGT records).
- **Infobox errors fixed**: Vaijapur 65,296→41,296; Paithan 57,280→41,536;
  Mancherial 228,450→89,935; Akola 621,000→425,817; Washim 212,400→78,387;
  Kothagudem 119,501→79,819; Karimnagar "497,447"→289,821. Warangal settled
  at M Corp 615,998 (not 830,281 expanded / 753,438 UA); Sircilla at
  M+OG 83,186; Shirdi 36,004 (conflicting 22,335 noted).
- **Jagtial MLA party: BRS, 2023** — he won as BRS, joined INC June 2024,
  but the Speaker dismissed the disqualification petition in Feb 2026
  ruling he remains a BRS legislator; Wikipedia's INC/"Elected 2024"
  infobox is wrong.
- **Beed MLA party: NCP-Sharadchandra Pawar** (not plain NCP/Ajit
  faction). **AP MLAs in Narasapuram and Polavaram (ST) won as Janasena
  Party**, not TDP (verified against ECI gazette No. 308/AP-LA/2024).
- **Kothapalli (Kataram) → Manthani AC, NOT Bhupalpalle**; Korutla town →
  Koratla constituency; Bellampally town → Bellampalli (SC); Armoor →
  Armur; Sultanabad → Peddapalle; Kanhan Pimpri → Savner (not Ramtek);
  Ardhapur & Mudkhed → Bhokar AC; Pauni → Sakoli; Ghuggus → Chandrapur
  (SC); Bhagur & Sansari → Deolali (SC).
- **Sultanabad MLA now MyNeta-verified**: Chinthakunta Vijaya Ramana
  Rao, INC, Peddapalle, 2023.
- No by-election since the last general election affects any of the 87
  seats; the only post-win party switch is Jagtial (above).
