# Cauvery industries — notes

## What this file is

8 aggregate rows for grossly polluting industries / CETPs in the Cauvery
basin. All rows are aggregates — no individual industry or CETP is listed,
because no published source gives per-unit discharge figures for the Cauvery
basin. **Effluent figures are consented / reported figures, never
measurements, and are never added to any drain's load.**

## The rows

### Karnataka (5 rows — from the Karnataka MPR Jan 2025)

- **Vrishabhavathi Valley industrial belt:** 191 industries (Red 31,
  Orange 60, Green 97, White 3). Effluent generation 8.008 MLD (sewage
  3.309 MLD, trade effluent 4.699 MLD). ~4.680 MLD of trade effluent is
  treated and discharged to land / gardening. **This is NOT a river
  discharge figure** — do not present it as one. No CETPs exist in the
  Arkavathi catchment.
- **Srirangapatna:** 6 industries, 1 water-polluting, effluent 0.0196 MLD,
  1 ETP (0.022 MLD capacity). Effluent generated, not river discharge.
- **Bannur:** 4 industries, 3 water-polluting, effluent 0.027 MLD, 3 ETPs
  (0.027 MLD).
- **T Narasipura:** 34 industries, 22 water-polluting, effluent 0.027 MLD,
  22 ETPs (0.027 MLD).
- **Kollegala:** 14 industries, 5 water-polluting, effluent 0.021 MLD,
  5 ETPs (0.021 MLD).

### Tamil Nadu (3 rows — from the TN MPR Feb 2021)

- **Trichy CETP:** 1 CETP, 7 units, reported discharge 201.4 KLD
  (0.2014 MLD), Complied.
- **Perundurai CETPs:** 2 CETPs, 14 units each; reported discharges
  1,000 KLD and 2,681 KLD (3.681 MLD total).
- **Tiruppur CETPs:** 18 CETPs; reported discharges 7,000; 180; 3,555;
  3,520; 2,750; 4,785; 3,748; 10,758; 2,250; 4,950; 4,500; 2,700; 165;
  2,750; 2,485; 4,500; 3,150; 1,350 KLD — **sum 65,096 KLD = 65.096 MLD**.
  **Caveat: most Tiruppur CETPs claim zero liquid discharge (ZLD), so the
  reported discharge figures need treating as reported, not verified.**
  The individual CETP figures are in the TN MPR Feb 2021 CETP table; the
  row here is the aggregate only.

## Gaps and caveats

- **No per-unit figures.** No individual industry or CETP discharge is
  published for the Cauvery basin in the sources used; hence aggregates
  only.
- **Effluent generated ≠ effluent discharged to the river.** The Karnataka
  figures are generation figures; the Vrishabhavathi trade effluent goes to
  land. The TN figures are reported discharges as stated in the MPR.
- **Proposed CETPs are not rows.** The 10 proposed CETPs (41 MLD,
  Rs 1,647.48 crore) in Erode/Namakkal are noted in
  `stp_budgets_NOTES.md` only — they are proposed, not built.
- **No industry data for** the Hemavathy, Shimsha, Kabini, Amaravathy or
  Noyyal tributary towns beyond the CETP aggregates above, and none for the
  delta reach.
- **No coordinates** are published for any industry or CETP in the sources
  used. Rows are plotted at their town centroids.
